Privacy Policy — ShareBee

Last updated: 23 September 2026
App: ShareBee (package: com.vtt.ShareBeeApp)
Contact: thuongvt2304@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy describes how the ShareBee mobile application (“ShareBee”, “we”, “the app”) handles information when you use the app.

1. Overview

ShareBee is a group bill-splitting tool. Project members, expenses, and payment QR settings are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a ShareBee account system and we do not require you to sign in.

2. Information we store on your device

  • Project names and dates
  • Participant count, member names, and host selection
  • Expense titles, amounts, and who paid
  • Optional bank code and account number you enter for VietQR transfer QR codes

This data stays on your device (local storage). Clearing app data or uninstalling ShareBee removes it, unless your device backs it up through the OS.

3. Network usage

ShareBee may use the internet to:

  • Load VietQR images from the VietQR image service based on the amount and account details you configured
  • Show advertisements, if AdMob / Google advertising is enabled in your build

We do not upload your bill projects to a ShareBee server.

4. Photos / media

If you choose to save a QR image, ShareBee may request permission to add an image to your photo library. We do not browse your photo library for other purposes.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

If the app displays ads via Google AdMob, Google may collect and process device and advertising-related information (such as advertising ID, IP address, and interaction with ads) according to Google’s policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • AdMob & privacy

You can reset or limit the advertising ID in your device settings (Google settings → Ads, or equivalent on your OS version).

6. Children’s privacy

ShareBee is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data retention & deletion

You control local data in the app (delete projects / clear settings). Uninstalling the app deletes app-local storage on that device.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of ShareBee after an update means you accept the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: thuongvt2304@gmail.com

Chính sách quyền riêng tư (tiếng Việt — tóm tắt)

ShareBee lưu dự án chia tiền, thành viên, khoản chi và cấu hình QR trên máy của bạn, không bắt buộc đăng nhập, không gửi dữ liệu dự án lên máy chủ ShareBee.
